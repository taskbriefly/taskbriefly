TaskBriefly Support

For help with TaskBriefly, TaskBriefly Pro, purchases, iCloud sync, shared categories, privacy, or account/data questions, contact:

taskbriefly.official@gmail.com

Please include your iOS version, TaskBriefly version/build and a short description of the issue. Do not send passwords, payment-card details or sensitive task content.

Subscriptions

TaskBriefly Pro subscriptions are billed and managed by Apple. You can manage or cancel an App Store subscription at Apple Subscriptions. Refund requests for App Store purchases are handled by Apple.

iCloud and Sharing

iCloud sync and shared categories depend on the Apple Account, iCloud settings, storage, network availability and Apple services. If you report a sharing issue, tell us whether the issue affects a personal category or a shared category and whether both participants are signed into iCloud.
